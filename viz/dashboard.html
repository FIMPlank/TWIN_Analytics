Twin Transformation: digitalization vs. verified emissions

Three views built from the open-access datasets in TWIN_Analytics/data/raw: EU ETS verified emissions (EUTL), the EIB Investment Survey (EIBIS), and the E-PRTR industrial emissions register.

EU ETS verified emissions by sector, 2005–2025

Independently verified emissions (not disclosed/self-reported) for the eight largest EU ETS sectors. Combustion of fuels dominates but has nearly halved since 2008.

Source: EU ETS / EUTL, GitHub mirror of EEA data · data/raw/eu_ets/eu-ets-sector-emissions.csv

Digitalization vs. climate-target adoption, EIBIS 2025

Each point is one EU country. X: share of firms using at least one digital technology. Y: share of firms with GHG emission-reduction targets. A positive slope is what the "digitalization → real decarbonization" hypothesis would predict — but EIBIS measures a stated target, not a verified outcome.

Source: EIB Investment Survey · data/raw/eibis/eibis_aggregate.csv (survey wave 2025, all sectors/sizes)

Industrial CO₂ releases by country, 2024

National totals from facilities reporting under the E-PRTR/Industrial Emissions Directive register — industrial CO₂ only, not full national inventories, and reporting completeness varies by country.

Source: E-PRTR / Industrial Emissions Portal · data/raw/eprtr/F1_1_Air_Releases_National.csv
